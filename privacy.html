Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

United Craftsman Services, LLC ("UCS," "we," "us") is a contractor in Columbus, Ohio, building decks, patios, stamped concrete, natural stone, pergolas and home additions. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you request a quote, we ask for:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your project address and zip code
  • Details about your project, your budget range and when you'd like to start
  • How you heard about us

We also keep what you share when you call, text or email us, including photos of your property or project, and the measurements, designs and notes we create while estimating and building your project.

Analytics and cookies (only with your permission)

Analytics tools load only after you accept analytics cookies. Before they load, we ask you; if you decline or don't answer, they never load. If you accept, we use one or both of these tools:

  • Google Analytics 4, from Google LLC, to count visits and see which pages people view, how they arrived (for example, from a search or a social media post), their general location (city level), the type of device and browser they use, and actions like submitting the quote form or tapping our phone number. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
  • Microsoft Clarity, from Microsoft Corporation, to see how visitors interact with our pages through heatmaps and session recordings of clicks, scrolling and mouse movement. Clarity masks text you type into form fields, so your answers are not recorded.

These tools set cookies in your browser, including:

  • _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics): tell visits apart; last up to 2 years.
  • _clck, _clsk, CLID and MUID (Microsoft Clarity): connect page views within a visit and across visits; last from 1 day to 1 year.

We use these tools only to understand and improve our website. We don't use them for advertising, and we never send your name, phone number, email, address or form answers to them.

Your choices: Once analytics is in use, you can change your answer at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. If you decline after accepting, we delete the analytics cookies we can reach. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as declining. You can also use Google's opt-out add-on. Your choice is saved in your own browser; clearing your browser data resets it.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and contact you about your project
  • To schedule consultations, prepare estimates and designs, and complete the work you hire us for
  • To arrange permits, inspections and utility marking for your project
  • To keep business, tax and warranty records
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it (only with your permission, as described above)
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Calls and text messages

When you submit a quote request, you agree that we may call or text you at the number you provide about your request and project. Texts may include scheduling, estimate and project updates. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts or HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Who processes your information

We share information only as needed to run our business:

  • Google LLC receives and stores quote requests through Google Forms and related Google services such as Google Sheets and Gmail, and runs Google Analytics if you accept analytics cookies. Google also provides the fonts on this site: your browser loads them from Google, which shares your IP address and basic browser details with Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Corporation runs Microsoft Clarity if you accept analytics cookies. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • HighLevel, Inc. (GoHighLevel) is the customer management system we use to track quote requests and follow up with you. A copy of each quote request, including your contact details and answers, is stored there. See the HighLevel Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel Inc. hosts this website and runs the service that passes your quote request to GoHighLevel. It processes basic technical data, such as IP addresses in server logs, to deliver and secure the site. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. stores this website's files and hosts a copy of the site through GitHub Pages, processing similar technical data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Project partners. Subcontractors, material suppliers, permitting offices and OHIO811 may receive the details they need to complete your project.
  • Legal reasons. If required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, our team or the public.
  • Business changes. If our business is sold or reorganized, customer records may transfer to the new owner.

Project photos

We photograph our work. When we share photos of completed projects, we leave out your name and street address and remove visible house numbers. See our Terms of Service for how to opt out.

How long we keep information

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as needed to respond to you, complete and warranty our work, and meet our tax and legal obligations. After that, we delete or securely dispose of them. Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including password-protected, access-controlled accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, unless we are required to keep it for legal, tax or warranty records. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This website is meant for adults planning home projects. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
