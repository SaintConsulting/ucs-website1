Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed November 1, 2026

United Craftsman Services wants everyone to be able to learn about our work and request a quote, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Project photos have text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Text and buttons use color contrast that meets WCAG AA.
  • Every form field has a clear label, required fields are marked, and error messages explain what to fix.
  • The project photo gallery opens and closes with the keyboard, and focus returns to the photo you opened.
  • The cookie choice banner has clearly labeled Accept and Decline buttons that work with a keyboard and screen readers, and it never blocks the rest of the page.
  • The site works on phones and when zoomed in, and it reduces motion for visitors who turn on that setting.
  • Pages use proper headings and landmarks so they're easy to navigate with assistive technology.

Known limitations

Our quote form sends your request to Google Forms, and our backup quote link opens a page that Google maintains. We can't control the accessibility of Google's pages. If any part of the form gives you trouble, call us and we'll take your request over the phone.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use or doesn't work with your assistive technology, please let us know. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll work to fix it and get you the information you need another way in the meantime.
